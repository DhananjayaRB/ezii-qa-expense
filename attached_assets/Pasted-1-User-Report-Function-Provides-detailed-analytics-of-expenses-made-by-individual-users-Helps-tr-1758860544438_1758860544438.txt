1. User Report

Function: Provides detailed analytics of expenses made by individual users. Helps track spending behavior, patterns, and trends at the employee level.

Requirement:

To monitor if employees are complying with company policies.

To identify high spenders or irregular claim patterns.

Useful for employee-level accountability and performance review.

2. Receipt Report

Function: Tracks receipts submitted against expenses for validation and auditing. Helps verify authenticity and prevent fraudulent claims.

Requirement:

To ensure all expenses are supported with valid proof (receipts/invoices).

Useful for finance and auditors to cross-check documentation.

Helps in tax compliance and reducing fraudulent expense claims.

3. Control Reports

Function: Offers insights into management controls, spending limits, budget adherence, and policy enforcement.

Requirement:

To maintain oversight and governance on overall expense processes.

To detect anomalies and policy violations early.

Helps management ensure compliance with internal financial policies.

4. Accountant Report

Function: Provides financial data for accounting, reconciliation, and audit purposes. Usually formatted for accountants and statutory compliance.

Requirement:

To prepare financial statements and ledger entries.

To support statutory audits and tax filing.

To ensure accurate financial reporting for the company.

5. Efficient Folks Report

Function: Tracks efficiency and performance of employees in expense management—how timely and accurate they submit claims, compliance rate, etc.
how claims are approved and payment completeing ,how many days late ,accurate or early details 

Requirement:

To recognize employees who handle expenses efficiently.

To improve productivity by spotting delays or inefficiencies.

Can be linked with employee rewards or performance reviews.

6. Department Report

Function: Analyzes expenses by department or cost center for budget allocation and cost control.

Requirement:

To monitor departmental budgets vs. actual spending.

To identify cost-heavy departments and optimize spending.

Useful for budget planning, forecasting, and management review.

7. Card Statement Analysis

Function: Matches and analyzes corporate card expenses against submitted claims. Ensures accurate reconciliation of company card usage.

Requirement:

To prevent misuse of corporate cards.

To reconcile company card statements with reported expenses.

Helps in fraud detection and ensuring transparency in card-based spending.

8. Compliance Report

Function: Tracks adherence to company expense policies and highlights exceptions/violations.

Requirement:

To ensure employees follow policies (limits, categories, documentation).

To reduce risk of non-compliance in audits.

Helps enforce discipline and transparency in spending practices.

✅ In short:

User & Efficient Folks → Employee-level insights.

Receipt & Compliance → Policy/audit validation.

Department & Control → Management-level oversight.

Card Statement & Accountant → Finance & reconciliation accuracy.
