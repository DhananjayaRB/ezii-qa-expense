Workflow Stages:

Employee → submits claim with expense details (category, amount, date, purpose, attachments like bills/receipts).

Manager → reviews, approves/rejects, or returns with remarks.

Admin → validates compliance with policies (limits, duplicates, correctness).

Head of Organization → provides final internal approval (optional, should be configurable).

Accounting Team → verifies and marks claim as Ready for Payment.

Payment Processing → Accounting team enters UTR number, clicks “Process Payment”, and claim moves to Processed Payments Summary.

Payment Processing Flow:

Once Accounting approves, the claim automatically appears in the Payment Processing Screen.

It shows claim details, employee details, approval trail, and bank details.

An empty input field for UTR number is provided.

After entering UTR and processing, the claim moves to Processed Payments Summary with: claim ID, employee name, payment date, UTR number, and processed by.

Transparency Features (Employee Dashboard)

Employees must have full visibility into their claim status at all times:

Current Status: Submitted / Pending / Approved / Rejected / Paid

Pending With: Name + Role of the approver (e.g., Pending with Manager – Mr. Rajesh)

Approval History: Timeline of all past approvals (e.g., Approved by Manager on 08-Sep, Approved by Admin on 09-Sep)

Remarks/Comments: Any notes added by approvers (e.g., “Bill corrected as per policy”)

Expected Next Step: Who is next in the workflow (e.g., Next approver – Accounting Team)

Progress Tracker (Visual): Submitted → Manager → Admin → Head → Accounts → Paid

Key Features Needed

Workflow approvals with audit trail at each step.

Configurable approver levels (some orgs may skip “Head” approval).

Role-based dashboards:

Employee → My Claims

Manager/Admin → Pending Approvals

Accounts → Payment Processing

Notifications/alerts (email + in-app) at each stage.

Export of processed payments to Excel/CSV for reconciliation.

Bulk payment processing option.

SLA reminders if claim is pending with any approver >3 days.

Suggested Improvements

Policy Check Engine: Auto-flag claims above limits or duplicate bills.

Document Attachments: Upload bills/receipts with each claim.

Mobile-Friendly Flow: Allow quick submission from phone.

Color-Coded Status: Pending = Yellow, Approved = Green, Rejected = Red, Paid = Blue.

Audit Log: Detailed logs for compliance and statutory audits.
