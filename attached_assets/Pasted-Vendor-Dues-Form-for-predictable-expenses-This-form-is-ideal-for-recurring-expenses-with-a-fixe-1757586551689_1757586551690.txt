Vendor Dues Form (for predictable expenses)

This form is ideal for recurring expenses with a fixed amount and due date, like rent or subscriptions. The system should automatically create a draft of this form based on a pre-configured schedule.

Trigger: A predefined schedule (e.g., monthly on the 1st of the month, or quarterly).
Form Name: Vendor Dues Form
Key Fields:
Vendor Name: (Auto-populated from the agreement)
Agreement/Contract Reference: (Auto-populated)
Expense Type: Rent, Lease, Subscription Fee (Auto-populated)
Period: (Auto-populated based on the schedule, e.g., "September 2025")
Due Date: (Auto-populated from the agreement)
Amount: (Auto-populated, editable if necessary)
Supporting Documents: A field to attach the initial agreement or any addendums.
Workflow: This form, once generated, enters the approval workflow for payment processing.
B) Utility Bill Form (for consumption-based expenses)

This form is specifically for bills where the amount is variable and based on consumption.

Trigger: A scheduled prompt or a user-initiated action upon receipt of the bill.
Form Name: Utility Bill Form
Key Fields:
Vendor Name: (e.g., Electricity Board, Water Department)
Account/Consumer Number:
Billing Period: (Start and End Date)
Reading Type: (e.g., Opening Meter Reading, Closing Meter Reading)
Consumption Units: (Calculated field based on the readings)
Bill Date:
Due Date:
Bill Amount:
Document Upload: A mandatory field to upload a clear image or PDF of the utility bill.
Workflow: Once the user inputs the data and uploads the bill, this form goes into the standard approval workflow.
C) Standard Vendor Bill Form

This is the most common form for one-time or milestone-based payments from a registered vendor.

Trigger: User-initiated upon receiving a bill from a vendor.
Form Name: Vendor Bill Form
Key Fields:
Vendor Name: (Selected from Vendor Master)
Invoice Number: (Mandatory field)
Invoice Date:
Due Date:
Invoice Amount:
Line Items: A table to break down the expenses (e.g., Description, Quantity, Rate, Total).
GST Details: A dedicated section for GST information, including the GSTIN, and a breakdown of SGST, CGST, and IGST.
Supporting Documents: An upload field for the invoice.
Workflow: This form proceeds through the standard approval process, which may include matching it against a PO.
D) Non-Registered Vendor Payment Form

This form is a variation of the standard bill form, with specific fields for managing payments to vendors who don't have a GST registration.

Trigger: User-initiated for payments to a non-registered vendor.
Form Name: Non-Registered Vendor Payment Form
Key Fields:
Vendor Name:
Transaction Details: A description of the service or goods received.
Amount:
PAN Number: This is a crucial field for TDS compliance if the amount exceeds the threshold.
TDS Section & Rate: The system should prompt the user to select the appropriate TDS section and automatically calculate the TDS amount.
Workflow: The form calculates the net payable amount and routes it for approval.
E) Purchase Order (PO) to Invoice Matching Form

This form is used when a vendor bill is received against a previously issued Purchase Order.

Trigger: User-initiated by linking a received vendor bill to an existing PO.
Form Name: PO Bill Matching Form
Key Fields:
Vendor Name: (Auto-populated from the linked PO)
PO Number: (Selected or auto-populated)
Invoice Number & Date:
PO Line Items: (Auto-populated)
Received Quantity: A field to input the quantity received against each line item.
Invoice Amount: A field for the bill amount.
Workflow: The system will validate if the invoice amount and received quantity match the PO. Any variance will trigger a specific approval route.
